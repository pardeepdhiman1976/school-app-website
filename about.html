About School App

A digital management solution for schools and preschools.

Who We Are

School App is a school and preschool management software initiative designed to help educational institutions organise their everyday administrative activities.

What We Aim to Do

Our aim is to make school management simpler by bringing important institutional information and activities together through a digital platform.

Our Mission

To support schools and preschools with accessible, easy-to-use digital management tools that help them organise their daily work.

Who Can Use School App?

Schools: For organising student, teacher and institutional information.

Preschools: For managing student records and everyday preschool information.
